# Phase 10 customer lifecycle foundation

The authenticated, read-only `/v1/operator/phase10/customer-lifecycle` view has `scope=live` (default) and `scope=lab`. It reads bounded Zoho Accounts, Contacts and Deals; a provider Account ID is the relationship anchor. It does not mint another customer ID. Lab scope requires registered IDs plus `OptiBrain_Test=true`; live scope excludes that marker. The existing Phase 9 source-attributed Account→Contact→Deal is reused as the active-project scenario, preserving its first source and campaign. No provider write route, outreach route, Books integration or autonomous job is added.

Deals already had `Service_Types`, `Stage` and source fields. Five new Deal fields hold facts the CRM lacked: `OptiBrain_Installed_On`, `OptiBrain_Last_Service_On`, `OptiBrain_Maintenance_Due`, `OptiBrain_Renewal_On`, and `OptiBrain_Recurrence`. They are dates or text, not financial amounts. A Deal with a completed stage and installation date is evidence of an installed service. A recurring cadence must be explicit; a service name alone does not claim a contract. Stage, service dates and explicit context drive one primary action per Account. Renewal review starts 45 Montreal calendar days before the recorded date; maintenance is due on its recorded Montreal date; dormancy requires completed installed work, at least 540 days without verified service, no active project and no recorded recurring service. Cross-sell needs completed cabling **and** documented wireless context without recorded Wi-Fi service. Camera expansion needs installed cameras **and** an explicit expansion or coverage-gap note. Healthy recent customers receive no outreach recommendation.

All CRM instants remain timezone-aware; operator times display in `America/Toronto`. Maintenance and renewal are business calendar dates in Montreal. `Last_Activity_Time` is shown separately from verified service history because CRM activity may only reflect editing. For protected historical Accounts without installed-service facts, the view returns `UNKNOWN` / `INSUFFICIENT DATA`, not a fabricated maintenance, dormancy or recurring obligation. Existing protected records remain read-only under the pinned 99-record manifest and single-use Test Lab transport boundary.

Root-only manual Test Lab evidence lives in `/var/lib/optibrain/phase10/test-lab/`. `ops/phase10/test_lab_lifecycle.py` journals every attempt before transport and never retries an ambiguous result. Seven new synthetic Account→Contact→Deal chains plus the Phase 9 chain prove eight lifecycle scenarios. Each object carries the existing `OptiBrain_Test` flag and visible marker. The script's `seed`, `negative`, and `restore` modes are separate manual actions. The dormant and maintenance negative transitions were read back from Zoho, then restored; a renewal moved from outside to inside the 45-day window. The append-only `lifecycle-events.db` records deterministic `OB-L-*` Test Lab events; a second sync added zero duplicates. It does not imply automated real-customer lifecycle event dispatch.

The recurring section lists service, cadence, start, renewal and review without synthetic value totals. It is a service pipeline, **not** booked recurring revenue. No Books record or real financial transaction was created. Phase 9 filtered Lead/Deal Zoho views still exclude tests; native Accounts/Contacts default views have no filtered operational counterpart yet. Use the OptiBrain live lifecycle view for customer counts until native filters are added, and do not use unfiltered Zoho Accounts/Contacts counts as business metrics.
