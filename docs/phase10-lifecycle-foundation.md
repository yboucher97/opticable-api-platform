# Phase 10 customer lifecycle foundation

The authenticated, read-only `/v1/operator/phase10/customer-lifecycle` view has `scope=live` (default) and `scope=lab`. It reads bounded Zoho Accounts, Contacts and Deals; a provider Account ID is the relationship anchor. It does not mint another customer ID. Lab scope requires registered IDs plus `OptiBrain_Test=true`; live scope excludes that marker. The existing Phase 9 source-attributed Account→Contact→Deal is reused as the active-project scenario, preserving its first source and campaign. No provider write route, outreach route, Books integration or autonomous job is added.

Deals already had `Service_Types`, `Stage` and source fields. Five new Deal fields hold facts the CRM lacked: `OptiBrain_Installed_On`, `OptiBrain_Last_Service_On`, `OptiBrain_Maintenance_Due`, `OptiBrain_Renewal_On`, and `OptiBrain_Recurrence`. They are dates or text, not financial amounts. A Deal with a completed stage and installation date is evidence of an installed service. A recurring cadence must be explicit; a service name alone does not claim a contract. Stage, service dates and explicit context drive one primary action per Account. Renewal review starts 45 Montreal calendar days before the recorded date; maintenance is due on its recorded Montreal date; dormancy requires completed installed work, at least 540 days without verified service, no active project and no recorded recurring service. Cross-sell needs completed cabling **and** documented wireless context without recorded Wi-Fi service. Camera expansion needs installed cameras **and** an explicit expansion or coverage-gap note. Healthy recent customers receive no outreach recommendation.

All CRM instants remain timezone-aware; operator times display in `America/Toronto`. Maintenance and renewal are business calendar dates in Montreal. `Last_Activity_Time` is shown separately from verified service history because CRM activity may only reflect editing. For protected historical Accounts without installed-service facts, the view returns `UNKNOWN` / `INSUFFICIENT DATA`, not a fabricated maintenance, dormancy or recurring obligation. Existing protected records remain read-only under the pinned 99-record manifest and single-use Test Lab transport boundary.

Root-only manual Test Lab evidence lives in `/var/lib/optibrain/phase10/test-lab/`. `ops/phase10/test_lab_lifecycle.py` journals every attempt before transport and never retries an ambiguous result. Seven new synthetic Account→Contact→Deal chains plus the Phase 9 chain prove eight lifecycle scenarios. Each object carries the existing `OptiBrain_Test` flag and visible marker. The script's `seed`, `negative`, and `restore` modes are separate manual actions. The dormant and maintenance negative transitions were read back from Zoho, then restored; a renewal moved from outside to inside the 45-day window. The append-only `lifecycle-events.db` records deterministic `OB-L-*` Test Lab events; a second sync added zero duplicates. It does not imply automated real-customer lifecycle event dispatch.

The recurring section lists service, cadence, start, renewal and review without synthetic value totals. It is a service pipeline, **not** booked recurring revenue. No Books record or real financial transaction was created. Phase 9 filtered Lead/Deal Zoho views still exclude tests; native Accounts/Contacts default views have no filtered operational counterpart yet. Use the OptiBrain live lifecycle view for customer counts until native filters are added, and do not use unfiltered Zoho Accounts/Contacts counts as business metrics.

## Phase 10 installed-service and contract extension

Zoho already has `Service_Locations` (lookup to Account/Contact) and `Services` (lookup to Service Location/Deal). These are the canonical business-level site and installed-service records; no parallel customer or asset module was created. Before any Service write, the 12 existing Services, 12 Service Locations and zero Installations were captured in a root-only protected snapshot at `/var/lib/optibrain/phase10/service-protected-baseline.json`. The original 99-record protected baseline is unchanged. The Test Lab write boundary checks both snapshots and registered owned IDs, including every relationship target. All pre-existing records in these modules remain read-only.

The Services module now has `OptiBrain_Test`, installation date, last service date, next maintenance date, renewal date, recurrence and recurring amount in CAD. Service Type, Service Stage, Contract Type, contract signed time, site and source Deal are reused. The existing Service Type picklist covers cabling, Wi-Fi, cameras, access control and VoIP; PTP, AI loss prevention and temporary jobsite examples use `Other` with an explicit service name until the native picklist is reviewed. One-time installations never become recurring merely because they have an installed date. An active recurring contract requires `Contract_Type=Recurring Service` and `Service_Stage=Active`; MRR/ARR require an explicit Monthly, Quarterly or Annual cadence and amount. Values are synthetic only for Lab records. Books remains read-only.

The authenticated `/v1/operator/phase10/recurring-services` route shares the lifecycle view's fresh CRM reads and `scope=live|lab` boundary. It shows customer, site, service, cadence, status, renewal, maintenance and known MRR/ARR. Unknown or incomplete live contract values remain unknown; no inferred revenue enters live totals. The customer-lifecycle route now incorporates Service evidence and retains linked Deal project context for cross-sell/upsell. An active recurring Service suppresses dormancy even when the last sales activity is old. All operator dates use Montreal business dates; CRM instants remain aware.

The 30-minute `opticable-phase10-service-events.timer` runs a non-AI, read-only Zoho projection and writes only an isolated local SQLite ledger under the API service data directory. Events cover installation, recurring start, maintenance due/completed and renewal due/overdue/renewed. State and deterministic evidence IDs prevent repeat events on replay. It has no CRM or Mail write path. The earlier manually synced 19-event ledger remains historical evidence; the new service ledger is separate and automatically refreshed. The root Test Lab registry records provider IDs, journaled mutations and the protected Service snapshot digest.

The actual protected CRM inventory has 12 Service records and 12 Service Locations, but no verified installation or contract dates in the new fields. The live projection therefore identifies existing service *records* without inventing installed assets, maintenance obligations or recurring revenue. The smallest future data-entry set is installed date, service type, site/Account and status; recurring services additionally need contract type, cadence, amount and renewal date. Last service and next maintenance date are needed for automated maintenance. Native Account/Contact `All` defaults still include tests; use the OptiBrain live view for real counts. Official CRM V8 custom-view metadata is read-only, so native filtered Account/Contact view creation remains an operator UI/configuration task unless a supported write mechanism is added.
