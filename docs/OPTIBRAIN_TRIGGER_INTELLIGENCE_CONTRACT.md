## Durable evidence and owner review

The existing `phase12-autonomy.db` stores additive `acquisition_trigger_current` and immutable `acquisition_trigger_versions`; no ninth database. Trigger identity is provider + native source record ID. Source versions, raw selected records, publication/closing dates, source URL, initial observation and verification times remain inspectable. Older versions cannot restore an old open tender. Unchanged rechecks do not create a new trigger/version. Current version is retained; history beyond12 versions is eligible for projection pruning after90days, with immutable phase/recovery receipts preserved. Dated terminal history retires from the rebuildable queue after365days. Current storage is bounded to2,000 triggers; capacity holds new ingestion explicitly rather than evicting active evidence.

Companies, triggers and shadow opportunities also use existing AcquisitionStore entities/facts/aliases. Same proven domain/native buyer maps to reusable company identity; contradictory existing aliases are held, never fuzzy merged. Project actors retain distinct roles. Permit applicant, architect and buyer are not equivalent.

Opportunity quality has visible timing/service/geography/relationship/recurring components. Evidence confidence is STRONG / MODERATE / TENTATIVE / INSUFFICIENT and exposes identity, actor and source verification independently. Average demand, CPC, project value and installation scope are UNKNOWN unless actually supplied. Service fit is EXPLICIT SCOPE or PROJECT-TYPE INFERENCE, never a claim of confirmed work.

Priority is ACT NOW / REVIEW / RESEARCH / WATCH / IGNORE. ACT NOW requires strong proof, a near deadline, fresh source coverage and current complete matchable CRM/Apollo identities. Tender export coverage older than3days cannot produce STRONG urgency or actionable Sales. Closed/expired/cancelled/awarded tenders are historical. FOREIGN / OTHER CANADA remain research storage and are excluded from the Québec queue. Language does not prove geography.

Sales receives at most5 STRONG resolved fresh shadow reviews. Unresolved companies/actors, stale/incomplete collision state, suppression and ambiguous relationships do not enter actionable Sales. Existing Apollo ownership/recent sends produce coordination recommendations; an existing customer/project is relationship work. No “clear to contact” result exists. Generic source research remains in Acquisition.

`/v1/operator/acquisition` shows a compact current trigger sample, confidence, geography, service fit, CRM/Apollo ownership, freshness, source version and missing evidence. Owner review uses existing append-only SalesFeedback, exact displayed version, authenticated owner and same-origin bounded POST. DO NOT CONTACT is sticky locally, never an Apollo suppression mutation. Review acceptance does not authorize CRM promotion. RESEARCH / READY FOR OWNER REVIEW are current states; APPROVED FOR CRM / PROMOTED remain future separately authorized lifecycle concepts with no executor in Phase30.

The queue links source IDs to matching existing market service/geography hypotheses without blending raw facts into keyword demand or asserting ICP specificity. [Keyword economics](OPTIBRAIN_KEYWORD_ECONOMICS_CONTRACT.md) remains optional supporting cache evidence: Google Basic is deferred by owner; Semrush/Ahrefs intentionally optional. Missing Windsor economics stays UNKNOWN / SOURCE UNAVAILABLE and cannot disable trigger discovery.

## Completion and future boundary

Complete for this scope means current public samples plus isolated fixtures prove source/version/freshness, separate actor/company identity, service fit, why-now, collisions/suppression, replay, queue/review and no effects, with focused/full regression, exact release and verified recovery. It does not mean all municipalities are covered or a natural sales result occurred. Trigger-to-outreach or CRM promotion requires a later explicit owner graduation; Phase32/33 are not started here.
