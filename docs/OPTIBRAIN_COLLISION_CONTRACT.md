# Prospect collision and ownership

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT — API1.24.0. Read-only collision checks cover native CRM Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Deals, Service Locations and Services plus saved Apollo accounts/contacts, active sequences, recent sends, replies and suppression. CRM remains canonical active business data; Apollo/Claude retain production outreach ownership.

Domain/native identity matches are deterministic. Shared domains do not establish an individual institutional unit; no fuzzy company-name matching clears a collision. CRM and Apollo statuses are separate: known matches remain known, but absence requires fresh complete observations and a matchable identity. CRM identity/business freshness is2hours; Apollo complete-inventory freshness is6hours. Incomplete/stale evidence remains UNKNOWN rather than NEW.

Explicit CRM Account→Contact bindings are revalidated against current records. Official company email/role evidence is independent corroboration, not authority to update CRM. Public contact information cannot clear an opt-out or Apollo collision. Exact sourced-email duplicates consolidate contact evidence; partial names and similar titles do not merge people. Existing customers remain expansion/cross-sell contexts, open Deals remain Deal context and active Apollo companies remain ACTIVE ELSEWHERE. Suppressed analytical records remain retained while execution is prohibited.

PROSPECTING_READY means identity/domain, relevant current role, target geography and collision evidence support owner research review. It does not imply cold-send eligibility, consent, deliverable email, available tender status or permission to enroll/contact. Sales requires its independent stronger current-event gate. No record is forced into Sales by enrichment.
