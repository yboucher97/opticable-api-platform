# Recurring Service lifecycle

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT. Books recurring invoices are canonical. OptiBrain observes their native profiles, generated Invoice history, customer/Account relationships, balance and payment state; it never creates, stops, resumes, reprices or cancels billing. Service status and financial status remain separate. An overdue Invoice creates attention, never Service cancellation.

A durable CRM Service belongs to a Service Location/Account independently of its original Deal. Existing installed, recurrence, maintenance and renewal fields are reused. No new CRM module/field or billing engine is introduced. An explicit installed date supports annual **internal price review**, not automatic pricing. Renewal review uses one stable attention identity per Service/date with90/60/30-day windows; maintenance requires an explicit recorded due date. Books profile end date means billing-end review, never inferred legal contract expiry. Missing legal dates remain unknown.

Native linkage requires Books customer→CRM Account and generated Invoice→native Finance Deal→one Service→Site, all agreeing. Multiple Services or missing Deal links require human selection. Optional root-owned `/var/lib/optibrain/lifecycle/recurring-links.json` can contain explicit owner-reviewed profile/customer/Account/Site/Service identities, review actor/time and proof. It is not populated by guesses or service-writable state. The owner selects business records; engineering records/validates IDs. Protected historical records remain read-only.

Live audit:23 Books profiles,19 active,4 stopped,4 independently mapped CRM Accounts. Historical CRM Services have no recurring flags/native profile links;0/23 profile-to-Service bindings are currently proven. One paid historical Invoice belongs to a different customer than the current profile, modified later; its current-profile association is quarantined and never used to transfer historical revenue/balance. An unpaid/current inconsistency blocks observation. Review native records before any repair; no financial or protected change was performed.

Hourly root GET observations produce a private snapshot and root-owned Today projection. Source timestamp remains explicit; stale/unavailable observations grant no authority. Protected Services can generate **read-only Today attention**, not provider Tasks or updates. Cancellation recorded in CRM suppresses future renewal/maintenance/review attention but leaves Books untouched and surfaces owner billing review. Multiple concrete support/billing incidents or an explicit cancellation request can produce retention attention; no speculative churn score. Review requests and contextual opportunities are drafts/Today actions only; automatic review/marketing sends OFF.

Operator: Today → Maintenance and renewal, or `/v1/operator/recurring` using normal owner Access. CRM remains the cockpit; use Zoho Finance/Books for financial decisions. Root cache `/var/lib/optibrain/lifecycle/business-observation.json` is rebuildable display state; `/run/optibrain-readiness/recurring.json` is display only. Existing internal emergency stop stops this observation runner; customer-only stop remains independent. Restore starts writers OFF; read snapshots never authorize effects.
