# Company and official-domain resolution

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT — API1.24.0. Company, domain, actor and collision confidence are independent. A source-native buyer ID establishes the named buyer; it does not establish its website. Domain evidence is OFFICIAL / SUPPORTED / LIKELY / UNKNOWN. Only supported organization evidence improves shadow readiness. No confidence state authorizes a provider write.

Reviewed enrichment receipts bind an existing prospect ID to its exact provider/native record IDs and dated HTTPS evidence. Government directories must explicitly link the named organization to its website. Company names, French language, guessed domains and search filters are insufficient. Shared government, municipal and corporate domains are scoped to departments/units and cannot merge unrelated buyers or prove their absence in CRM/Apollo.

Multiple actors remain distinct. An exact address match to a tenant/operator identifies that role, not the permit applicant, property owner, commissioning buyer or contractor. Project placeholders remain durable after partial actor resolution. Actor coverage and unresolved commissioning buyers are reported separately. Historical events remain historical.

Native CRM Account→Contact references plus the same contact email on the official company site can link equivalent research identities. A matching company name cannot. The canonical prospect retains source identifiers; original prospect rows and proof receipts remain stored. Conflicting sites or names remain research holds. Company suppression, existing customers and active Apollo ownership survive consolidation.
