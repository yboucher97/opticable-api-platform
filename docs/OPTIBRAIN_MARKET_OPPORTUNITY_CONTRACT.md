# Market opportunity decisions

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT. `acquisition_intelligence.py` combines source evidence into15 initial service × ICP × geography targets. These are market-based hypotheses, not proven ROI or assertions that all observed queries/companies belong to the selected ICP/city. Primary geography is Montréal/Laval/Rive-Nord/Greater Montréal; Québec is secondary. User-defined service fit and recurring potential are explicit policy inputs.

An opportunity contains service, ICP, target geography, observed property query impressions, total market volume if measured, commercial intent, CPC, organic/paid difficulty, observed ranking, content/competitor evidence, company/contact coverage, recent dated triggers, recurring potential, confidence, explainable component points, priority, recommended channels and missing data. Null means unavailable. Search Console impressions describe Opticable visibility, not all searches in the market. Domain overlap alone does not prove current customer/project ownership. SEAO geography remains UNKNOWN unless a native record proves it; it cannot inflate city-specific permit priority.

Deterministic points combine intent, measured demand/own visibility, buying triggers, known addressable companies, service fit, strategic geography and recurring potential. Every HIGH opportunity carries source proof, why this market, why now, service/location/channel and missing fields. Generic informational demand ranks below commercial acquisition. Large company coverage with very little measured search demand favors SHADOW outbound research. Relevant search visibility can favor existing-page SEO/content/local work and owner-reviewed future paid research. No CPC or profitability assumption is substituted for missing economics. All opportunities deny automatic CRM promotion, cold sends and campaign mutation; existing Claude/Apollo ownership removes competing outbound recommendations.

Start in ZERO-LEAD MARKET EVIDENCE mode. Future exact verified, TEST-excluded cohorts may influence priority only after at least10 observations and valid counts. Outcome weight increases with verified sample size, capped at80%; unsupported or tiny samples have zero outcome weight. This is an interface for later trustworthy feedback, not fabricated current conversions or an opaque prediction.

Current scores are policy ordering. Search demand/CPC/difficulty/contact coverage remain missing where providers do not expose them. Content opportunities narrow this model to an actual existing page or an owner-reviewed new-page candidate; see [SEO contract](OPTIBRAIN_SEO_INTELLIGENCE_CONTRACT.md). Business action and outbound ownership remain in CRM/Today Sales.

## Evidence confidence and geography

Completion remediation separates opportunity signal from evidence support. Average position14 at1 impression earns a smaller positional contribution than14 at100 impressions; owner text includes sample/window. A high priority cannot rely on a tiny sample alone. Independent measured demand may corroborate it without relabeling Search Console evidence strong. Fact objects distinguish MEASURED FACT, DERIVED FACT, ESTIMATE, INFERENCE and UNKNOWN; missing metrics remain null, real zero stays zero.

Geographic states include CANADA, QUÉBEC, MONTRÉAL, LAVAL, RIVE-NORD, GREATER MONTRÉAL, QUÉBEC CITY, OTHER CANADA, FOREIGN and UNKNOWN. Seed segments are target hypotheses. Foreign/other-Canada queries are retained for diagnostics and excluded from Québec SEO/paid queues. Canadian impressions do not prove a city/ICP market. Current version/status, actor proof and freshness accompany project signals; unresolved company/contact recommendations stay RESEARCH NEEDED. The current two SEAO buyers are confirmed from native IDs plus their official domains and current public pages, with September30 addenda; their employees still require role verification.
