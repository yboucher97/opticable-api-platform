# Sales conversation intelligence

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT — Phase34 / API1.27.0. Read/analyze/prepare only. Existing [sales coexistence](OPTIBRAIN_SALES_INTELLIGENCE_CONTRACT.md) remains the ownership and collision policy.

The canonical Conversation reuses the existing `phase12-autonomy.db`, table `sales_conversation_versions`: stable person/channel identity, immutable consecutive revisions and exact replay. Exact normalized email or native IDs can join provider contacts; similar names cannot. Employer domain can link company context, never establish a person match. Fields include provider/person/company references, exact CRM/Apollo references, first/last observed contact and reply time, intent, urgency, service/trigger/strategy, exclusive owner, next action, suppression, separate contact/role confidence, pressure, provenance and verification date. Observed first contact and touch count are bounded lower limits, not full history. No ninth database or business record is created.

Owners: APOLLO_CLAUDE, OPTIBRAIN_FUTURE, OWNER_MANUAL, CRM_INBOUND, CUSTOMER_RELATIONSHIP, OTHER. Every saved Apollo relationship retains Apollo/Claude ownership, including paused/completed membership. A partner/supplier reply is not a buyer inquiry. Existing Account/Contact context alone does not prove paid-customer history. Open Deal/Estimate/revenue links require exact native associations; missing Finance lineage is UNKNOWN.

Reply classes: POSITIVE_INTEREST, ASKING_QUESTION, MEETING_INTEREST, QUOTE_INTEREST, REFERRAL, FOLLOW_UP_LATER, NOT_NOW, NOT_INTERESTED, WRONG_PERSON, LEFT_COMPANY, AUTO_REPLY, OUT_OF_OFFICE, UNSUBSCRIBE, BOUNCE, OTHER, UNKNOWN. Deterministic versioned rules strip HTML/style and quoted outbound material before classification. Automated headers and negative intent take precedence. Ambiguous two-word forwards stay tentative/research-only. Apollo reply/Interested flags without a body do not establish positive intent. Urgency HOT/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/NONE is distinct from qualification; partner meetings are HIGH, not customer quotes. Older useful replies require current-context review rather than invented urgency.

Mail uses the existing authenticated account and exact known Apollo senders, dates and Internet Message-ID references. A body is read only after exact sender/recipient/header linkage. Unmatched, capped or failed reads retain an explicit gap; no body is invented. Full source content remains root-only. The owner receives bounded private excerpts, previous-message context and actual unsent drafts. Git evidence contains aggregates and non-sensitive hashes only.

Pressure records last observed outreach, a 30-day touch-count lower bound, memberships and last reply. Manual contact and unobserved history remain NOT CLEARED. No automatic follow-up date is inferred from a stale availability message. Next actions are specific: REPLY NOW, RESEARCH FIRST, OWNER REVIEW, WAIT UNTIL DATE, PREPARE QUOTE, CUSTOMER EXPANSION REVIEW, NO ACTION or SUPPRESS; this representation never executes them.

`/v1/operator/sales` and existing Today reuse authenticated Cloudflare Access/human verification, no-store responses and escaped content. Today receives at most three high-value shared priorities, never the research backlog. Preview review is exact-revision/hash and same-origin; REVIEWED/RESEARCH/REJECT remain local feedback, not execution approval. No send route is added.

Signature/footer questions are excluded. An actual NDA/contract step is classified as human-controlled owner review (ClassD); no signing or legal judgment. Earlier exact-thread project context remains separately dated, not a claim that the latest administrative reply requests a new quote.
