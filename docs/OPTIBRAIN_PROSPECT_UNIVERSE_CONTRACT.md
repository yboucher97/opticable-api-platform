## Pools and readiness

Logical pools include SALES ACTIONABLE, PROSPECTING READY, NEEDS CONTACT, NEEDS COMPANY RESOLUTION, RESEARCH, WATCH, HISTORICAL INTELLIGENCE, SUPPRESSED, EXISTING CUSTOMER, ACTIVE DEAL, ACTIVE APOLLO and TERMINAL IGNORE. One organization can belong to historical/watch/contact-gap pools simultaneously; primary pool counts are exclusive, secondary counts overlap. A native buyer identity can be EXACT while its domain/contact/collision is unknown. Generic fit and current WHY NOW are different. No current event is labeled NO CURRENT TRIGGER; no urgency is invented.

Missing domain/contact/timing/confidence never alone causes terminal ignore. True ignore needs an explicit relevant reason, timestamp, source and FINAL/REVISITABLE policy. Suppression retains analytical intelligence while preventing execution. Unknown fit is research; confirmed irrelevant scientific/sewer/noncommercial records may be terminal. Commercial premises can remain plausible research even if the older event scope classifier finds no explicit service.

Prospect state preserves name/domain/location/industry where supplied, source provenance, first/last seen, trigger history, service rationale, contact coverage, CRM/Apollo/customer/Deal/suppression state, explainable research components and next research date. Missing values stay unknown. ICP-only native companies and primary-source companies are supported independently of current permits/tenders.
