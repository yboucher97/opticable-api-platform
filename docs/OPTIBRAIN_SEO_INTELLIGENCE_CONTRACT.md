# SEO intelligence

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT. `seo_intelligence.py` inventories live first-party pages and prepares decisions, not mass publication. Owner view is [Acquisition](https://optibrain.opticable.ca/v1/operator/acquisition).

The Phase29 bounded inventory read135 own URLs from main/AI sitemaps and roots. All were crawlable/indexable candidates in this sample. Native URL Inspection proved indexing for two commercial Wi-Fi language pages only; unknown indexing for other URLs is not converted into indexed or deindexed. Sitemaps' legacy indexed count is not authoritative full coverage. No deterministic issue remained in the inspected HTML checks after ordinary redirect aliases were excluded. Page/mobile performance, complete external orphan coverage and uninspected pages are unmeasured; absence of a bounded issue is not a universal SEO clean bill.

Inventory retains title, description, H1/H2, canonical, language/hreflang, robots/indexability, sitemap evidence, JSON-LD, internal/external links, page type, service/context, CTA/forms and visible script instrumentation. Crawling is limited to own HTTPS hosts, respects robots/delay, bounds redirects, bytes and concurrency, and does not follow external links. Page metadata is observation; HTML scripts do not prove successful analytics events. No structural production site change was made.

FR and EN are independent. Native query evidence plus21 separately phrased service seed families cover cabling/Cat6/Cat6A, fiber, Wi-Fi, networks, cameras, access/intercom/alarm, PTP, temporary construction connectivity/cameras, telephony, managed support and AI loss prevention. Seeds have no manufactured volume/CPC/ranking. Language UNKNOWN is held; no page is substituted across languages. Keyword classification is transactional, commercial investigation, local service, informational, branded or irrelevant.

For each query, the native ranking URL is stronger than topic similarity. A suitable existing service/language page is improved before creating another. A near-page-one boost requires actual impressions/position8–20 on that selected URL, not another page's or language's position. Query/page evidence remains visible separately from target geography hypotheses. New-page candidates require distinct user value and owner expertise/demand validation; city doorway expansion is denied. Informational/irrelevant queries do not drive mass article plans.

Competitors were discovered through current FR/EN service searches and checked primary pages:10 targets, seven readable. Self-described service overlap is baseline evidence; neutral Google rank, keyword gaps, backlink authority, paid share and changes over time remain unmeasured. No competitor content is copied. Detailed Semrush/Ahrefs tools are unavailable in this execution, and Keyword Planner rejects Explorer access; exact states appear in [sources](OPTIBRAIN_MARKETING_DATA_SOURCES.md). Future direct Search Console data outranks third-party estimates.

Paid research suggests service/language campaign families, high-intent themes, landing gaps and irrelevant negative themes. Missing CPC is unknown. Weak observed organic coverage may justify coordinated SEO plus later paid testing; all campaign decisions remain HUMAN. GBP/local recommendations use genuine project photos, service/category consistency and meaningful local proof, not auto-posting or duplicate city pages.
