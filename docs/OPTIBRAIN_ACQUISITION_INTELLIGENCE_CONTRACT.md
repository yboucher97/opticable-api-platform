## Storage and identities

`acquisition_store.py` adds rebuildable acquisition tables to the existing `phase12-autonomy.db`. There is no new production database or warehouse. Entities, exact aliases, compressed source snapshots, immutable normalized/raw facts, conflicts, source health and daily metric history are separate. COMPANY, PERSON, LOCATION, PROJECT, TRIGGER, KEYWORD, SEARCH_TOPIC, SERP_RESULT, COMPETITOR/PAGE, CONTENT_TOPIC/ASSET, OPTICABLE_PAGE, AD_KEYWORD/SEARCH_TERM/COMPETITOR, MARKET_SEGMENT, ICP, SERVICE, SOURCE, OBSERVATION, OPPORTUNITY, OUTREACH_STATE, SUPPRESSION and SOURCE_PERFORMANCE are supported types. Unsupported source families remain empty rather than invented. The SERVICE vocabulary describes acquisition markets; it does not create installed CRM Services.

Company identity uses exact native source IDs, verified domain, NEQ/registry number, Apollo organization ID, Clay company ID or CRM Account ID. Shared social/free-email hosts and similar company names are not company identity. Person identity uses normalized exact email/native contact IDs; names alone never merge people. Locations retain exact normalized address, unit, postal code and native Site ID. Keywords retain query, language and geographic scope. Projects/triggers retain source/native record ID. A bridge between two existing identities is held for human review; no fuzzy merging or silent conflict replacement.

Every fact retains source, native ID, URL, raw/normalized value, storage `retrieved_at`, actual provider `observed_at` where known, effective date, confidence, cost class and snapshot proof. Storage time is not a new provider observation. First-party/native evidence has priority over broker or third-party estimates, with conflicting facts retained. Tokens/passwords/credential URLs are denied. Raw business contacts are root-only; owner projection exposes no person directory or raw tracking IDs. TEST_ONLY records are excluded.
