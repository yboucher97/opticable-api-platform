# Outreach intelligence and preparation

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT — Phase34 / API1.27.0. Uses [shared proposals](OPTIBRAIN_OPTIMIZATION_PROPOSAL_CONTRACT.md), [conversations](OPTIBRAIN_SALES_CONVERSATION_CONTRACT.md), [priorities](OPTIBRAIN_BUSINESS_PRIORITY_CONTRACT.md) and [authority classes](OPTIBRAIN_AUTHORITY_CLASSES.md). Claude/Apollo production remains untouched.

Preparation is broad; execution stays contact-verified, role-aware, suppression-aware, recent-contact-aware and bounded. Strong ICP/historical/repeat buyers remain eligible for drafts without a current trigger: WHY NOW = NO CURRENT TRIGGER. Supported roles may support role-addressed previews even when exact delivery channels need verification. PREVIEW_READY does not mean send-ready. Existing Apollo relationship ownership always blocks competing cold execution preparation. Customers receive relationship/expansion review, never cold framing. Secondary roles are retained; they are not contacted simultaneously.

Proposal types SALES_REPLY, OUTREACH_PROPOSAL, CUSTOMER_EXPANSION, SEQUENCE_CHANGE and CONTENT_FEEDBACK instantiate the existing immutable shared schema/store. Targets use existing OUTREACH/SALES_PROCESS/CONTENT categories. Drafts preserve language evidence; FR preparation defaults are explicitly owner-confirmation-required, not geography-based language inference. Service-specific copy, subject, role, current/historical context, first message, follow-up strategy, evidence, collision/suppression/owner, contact confidence, uncertainty and measurement are reviewable. Missing dates, price, claims and contact details are never invented. A question outside verified technical context produces an investigation draft, not a fabricated answer.

The existing five-minute internal observer reuses its six-hour complete Apollo identity/workspace cache and fresh CRM observations. New reply lookups are capped at four known contacts, sixteen GETs and forty seconds per cycle; exact per-query success cooldown is six hours, failed/unmatched cooldown twenty-four hours. A root non-blocking lock prevents overlapping preparation. Existing STOP plus CONVERSATIONS_STOP stop this domain. Dry runs make no new sales provider reads or preparations. No new scheduler, persistent model, paid enrichment, or runtime LLM call. Immutable original Forms receipts and journals are separate and untouched.

Private caches: `/var/lib/optibrain/sales-intelligence/conversation-mail.json`, `conversation-bundle.json`, `conversation-sequences.json`. Root-only source evidence and failed lookup result/cooldown are durable. Display: `/run/optibrain-readiness/sales-conversations.json`, root/API-group0640, bounded250KB; freshness is checked by the existing owner-view reader. Collection and preparation origins are distinct UNATTENDED/MANUAL/UNKNOWN; TEST and genuine effects are separate. Detailed period/variant analytics presently use a bounded native connector snapshot, not an unattended analytics refresh. Old snapshots expire after seven days; absent current data lowers confidence.

Future graduation only: SHADOW → AUTO DRAFT → OWNER APPROVE EXACT BATCH → SEALED APOLLO OPERATION → LIMITED OBSERVE/MEASURE → CONTINUE/STOP. Required seal: proposal revision/hash, exact recipient IDs, exact message hash/channel/sequence, existing exclusive owner transfer, suppression/CRM/recent/manual checks immediately before execution, current employer/role evidence, daily/org cap, expiry, approval receipt, kill switch, idempotency, read-after-write and unknown-effect HOLD. No generic send/edit API. `outreach_graduation.py` only validates a candidate; execution is unconditionally denied even when a fixture is approved. No outreach mutation authority is installed.

Positive-reply handoff is a proposal to coordinate existing Apollo ownership, review continued sequence pressure, reconcile CRM, assign owner and track the next step. It does not pause/remove contacts. Outcome learning distinguishes delivered/replied/positive/qualified/Deal/Estimate/accepted/paid; deterministic native links only. No winner is claimed from opens or tiny A/B samples. Recurring support/maintenance potential has unknown scope, price and margin until verified. Sales questions can prepare FAQ/content/website proposals; Ads cross-learning never implies Ads attribution for an Apollo reply.
