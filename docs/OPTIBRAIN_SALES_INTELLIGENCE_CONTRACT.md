# Sales intelligence and prospecting coexistence contract

AUTHORITATIVE CURRENT. CRM is business truth; Books is financial truth. Apollo remains production outreach execution; Claude remains the owner-designated research/personalization controller. [Observed flow](CURRENT_CLAUDE_APOLLO_FLOW.md) separates observation from inference. OptiBrain new prospecting is **SHADOW ONLY**.

## Boundary and ownership

`ApolloReader` permits only fixed saved-contact/account/campaign/message/label/stage read endpoints. Some documented searches use POST; they remain read-only. No sender, sequence enrollment/update, contact creation, enrichment purchase or generic destination exists. The legacy generic Apollo client retains its credit guard. Fixed 32-call/40-second refresh budget, 2,000-contact ceiling, complete unique pagination; changed/incomplete populations fail closed.

Exact normalized email/native IDs and independently verified employer domains identify collisions. Existing Apollo contacts and saved accounts belong to `CLAUDE_APOLLO`, including paused/completed or previously contacted prospects. A company-domain collision is a conservative company hold, **not** proof two people are identical. CRM Contacts/Accounts identify existing customer context; duplicate native candidates remain ambiguous. No fuzzy merges.

Unsubscribe, DNC, Not Interested, invalid/deleted/changed-job data, known bounce/spam and unverified email suppress new outreach. All shadow contact recommendations and sends remain disabled even on no-match: recent Zoho Mail, complaints and consent require human checks. Absent bounded activity is unknown, not no contact. Existing operational customer families retain their separate approved policies; these changes add no cold-mail scope and do not control Apollo.

## Sources and ICP

Montréal commercial/industrial permits provide native project event/date/address, not company identities. SEAO OCDS tenders provide native buyer/project/version/deadline; latest release wins, closed/expired/irrelevant scientific/sewer/fire-only projects excluded. Verify latest amendment and procurement terms before acting. No project-to-company guess. Public source is cited on every review item; potential service need remains a hypothesis. No PII in analytics.

Useful ICP families remain separate: property managers/developers/multi-residential/seniors residences; pharmacies/retail/dépanneurs/gas stations; warehouse/industrial/offices/yards; auto/dealerships; construction/general contractors/electrician partners. Classify only with source evidence. Cabling/fiber/WiFi/network, cameras/access/intercom, PTP/construction connectivity, telephony/managed services, AI loss prevention or maintenance fit must be specific to the trigger, not every service for every company.

Apollo saved data is reused before research. Two zero-credit API searches demonstrate buyer-domain discovery; limited search results do not prove current employer, role or reveal emails. Paid enrichment remains off. Clay is supplemental only for a known missing field, with a callable connection and bounded credit approval/account limits verified; current provider comparison deferred. Quebec business-register/expansion feeds are optional future sources; no unsupported scraping or mass creation.

Optional observer failure creates a local exception and does not disable intake, CRM lifecycle, Finance observation or Apollo. No new timers/services/write scopes.
