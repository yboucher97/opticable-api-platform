# Observation and completeness contract

Implementation contract for API 1.29.0. Production status is established by the exact release receipt. The [PR133 recovery report](pr133-release-recovery/final-report.md) records the verified matching release/recovery, G004 closure and G001 natural-verification limit; historical release evidence is preserved. See the [deployment guide](OPTIBRAIN_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md) and [runtime contract](OPTIBRAIN_RUNTIME_CONTRACT.md).

The existing schema3 Business observation gains `attempted_at`, `collection`, `view_sources`, `last_good_modules`, and `detail_checkpoint`. Existing `snapshot`, `recurring`, `marketing`, and `observed_at` readers remain supported. Each stage/module records state, completeness, attempted/successful/failed/provider reads, source time, observation time, allocated budget where applicable, and sanitized error type/status. Failed attempts preserve the independently retained complete module. Missing modules remain unknown. Legacy schema3 caches seed only present atomic modules and identifiable full details; a recurring-only customer subset cannot establish complete customer context.

The lifecycle budget stays at 160 reads, including reads already consumed by lifecycle work. The collector reserves up to three existing sales identity reads, allocates 45% of the remaining required-stage budget to Marketing and 55% to recurring collection, then allows at most three optional reads and eight finance delta reads from remaining capacity. Budget rejection makes no provider request. Attempt counts include rejected requests; successful/failed read counts describe provider responses. Required stages expose exhaustion explicitly and cannot consume another required stage's reservation.

Native ID, aware modified-version evidence and unchanged customer identity permit exact detail reuse. The existing finance reuse follows this same comparison. A current listing verifies an unchanged version; the cached detail's original source date and native record dates stay intact. Customer inventory includes customers without financial transactions. Generated invoice histories are read independently on every recurring refresh; profile modification alone never proves history unchanged. Successful details survive an interrupted module and support bounded progress on the next ordinary hourly refresh. Each listing remains limited to five pages of 200, and the detail checkpoint is limited to 4,000 entries. No provider writes, LLM calls, scheduler changes or persistent worker are introduced.

The aggregate generation time is separate from source times. Business, Marketing and Manager projections expose partial/failed/stale module states and retain the source date of an older complete view. Optional failures preserve required modules. Business HTML and CSV include collection coverage; neither a refreshed projection nor a current identity-only read certifies the whole source.

CRM collision inputs carry independent completeness for Leads, Contacts, Accounts, Deals, Services, Sites and deterministic customer context. Customer context requires complete Accounts, Services, Sites and Books customer evidence. A source must have both collection proof and a present list to establish verified emptiness. The CRM decision tolerance is two hours; the Apollo tolerance is six hours. Missing, failed, partial and expired sources cannot establish negative clearance and hold readiness. Known collisions, relationships and suppression remain visible and held.

Apollo completeness is independent for saved contacts, accounts, sequences, stages, ownership, suppression, messages and replies. Complete contacts do not prove complete activity. A bounded activity response without explicit complete pagination remains partial. Ownership and suppression require the fields used by decisions. Unsupported provider coverage stays unknown/partial. The existing collision engine serves triggers, Prospect Universe, enrichment and sales; no second engine or send authority is added.

The local sampler classifies each service invocation as RUNNING, COMPLETED_SUCCESS, FAILED, TIMED_OUT, NEVER_RUN, STALE_COMPLETION or UNKNOWN. Running invocations use a nonzero monotonic start and the applicable timeout, without completion staleness from exit zero. Successful completed oneshots use nonzero start/exit evidence; ActiveEnter zero is valid. Malformed, future and inconsistent monotonic evidence is unknown. Timer activity and authority determine expected jobs separately. The sampler never starts or restarts jobs.

Validation uses fake adapters and isolated cache files under the existing network-blocked regression gate. Old phase receipts, protected provider records, authority scopes, cutoffs, expiry, counters, remote claims, conversion-export flags and English automation policy are unaffected by this contract.

The release staging helper is [stage_runtime.py](../ops/post37_remediation/stage_runtime.py), adapted from the existing guarded transaction into a new evidence directory. It additionally binds validation to the sampler hash. Install/run it only from the reviewed immutable release after exact-main CI and the normal gate prerequisites. Apply the root-installed sampler from that same commit using deployment guide step 7, preserving its previous bytes. Neither helper starts a job to manufacture collection evidence. During PR133 recovery only `--prepare` was used; `--activate` would restore authority and was intentionally omitted. Matching recovery and G004 are verified in the current recovery report; G001 awaits natural verification with explicitly authorized internal execution.
